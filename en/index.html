Questions and answers

Grodus Wallet: frequently asked questions

How do I open Grodus Wallet?

Open the official link https://t.me/GrodusWalletBot or search for @GrodusWalletBot in Telegram and tap Start.

Which networks and cryptocurrencies are supported?

Grodus Wallet supports TON, TRON, Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain and Polygon, the native assets TON, TRX, SOL, ETH, BNB and POL, and USDT on supported networks.

Does it require a seed phrase?

No. Grodus Wallet uses a custodial model, so users do not need to create or manually record a seed phrase inside the bot.

Does Grodus Wallet have a separate website or app?

This page is the official public information documentation. The wallet itself operates only through @GrodusWalletBot; there is no separate wallet app or web wallet.
